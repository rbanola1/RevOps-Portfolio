# Sales compensation plan (illustrative, best-practice design)

## Structure
| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| On-target earnings (OTE) | $150,000 |
| Pay mix | 60% base ($90,000) and 40% variable ($60,000) |
| Commission rate at 100% of quota | Target variable divided by quota; about 6.6% on a $0.91M quota |
| Below 50% of quota | Paid at half rate |
| 50% to 100% of quota | Full rate |
| 100% to 150% of quota | 1.5x accelerator |
| Above 150% of quota | 2x accelerator |
| Cap | None on commission; a leadership review applies above 200% |

## What is paid on
- Booked revenue on new customers and expansion at the full rate.
- Distributor and marketplace orders in a territory: shared credit, set in the partner agreement and written in the plan, so the same sale is not paid twice.
- Renewals and re-orders from existing customers at a lower rate, if the plan covers them.

## Supporting rules
| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Paid on cash received or on invoice, not on a verbal order | Ties pay to revenue the company keeps |
| Clawback if an order is cancelled or refunded within 90 days | Protects against bad deals |
| Payment monthly or quarterly, with a clear statement | Reps can check their own pay |
| Ramp: guaranteed draw or reduced quota for the first two quarters | Fair to new hires |
| Spiffs for a launch or a strategic product, limited to one quarter | Short-term focus without changing the plan |
| Plan documents signed each year | Removes disputes |
| Adjustments for territory changes are made in writing | Fairness when accounts move |

## Checks before the plan is issued
- Cost of sales (total pay divided by revenue) in the range the budget allows; the synthetic run gives about 15%.
- Share of reps above target roughly 50% to 60%.
- Pay at 50% attainment is meaningfully below OTE but not zero, so effort is still rewarded.
- Finance and HR review before the plan is shared; legal reviews the clawback wording.

## Note
The figures are examples from common practice for a small life-science tools team, not the terms of any real company's plan.
