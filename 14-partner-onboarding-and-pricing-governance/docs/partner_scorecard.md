# Partner scorecard

| Measure | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue, last 12 months | 30% | Order system |
| Sell-through | 25% | Partner reports and orders against stock or listings |
| Catalog accuracy | 20% | Monthly catalog audit |
| Order errors | 15% | Order corrections as a share of orders |
| Response time | 10% | Average days to answer quotes and questions |

## Tiers and actions
| Score | Tier | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 65 to 100 | Grow | Joint marketing, event support, first access to new products |
| 40 to 64 | Maintain | Quarterly check-in; fix the lowest-scoring measure |
| Below 40 | Review | Improvement plan with dates; reduce support if no change in two quarters |

## Rules
- Scores are shown to partners with the measures behind them.
- A partner is reviewed with a written plan before any tier change that reduces support.
- Scores are recalculated each quarter from the same sources.
