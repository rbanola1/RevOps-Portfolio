# Pricing and catalog sheet standard

## One master, many partner sheets
The master sheet holds one row per product with these fields. Partner sheets are produced from it.

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| SKU | One SKU per product everywhere; changes are made in the master first |
| Description | Same wording and pack size on every partner |
| Unit of measure | The partner's required code, mapped from the master unit |
| List price | From the approved price list; partner discounts are separate columns |
| Lead time | In days, in the partner's required format |
| Trade codes and classification codes | Owned by the logistics and operations team; not edited by commercial staff |
| Last updated | Date and name of the person who changed it |

## Process
1. Read the partner's own guidelines before filling anything.
2. Build the partner sheet from the master; do not retype values.
3. Run the cross-partner check: SKU, price, pack size and lead time compared for every product on every partner.
4. Send, then record the date sent and the date the listing went live.
5. Any price or product change is made in the master, then pushed to all partners and logged.

## Cross-partner check
A mismatch is any product whose SKU, price, pack size or lead time differs from the master or from another partner without a recorded reason (for example, an approved partner discount).
