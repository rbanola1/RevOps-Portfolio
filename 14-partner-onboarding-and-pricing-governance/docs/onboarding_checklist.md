# Partner onboarding checklist

| Step | Tasks | Owner | Target days |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Due diligence and fit | Check territory overlap, customer fit, financial standing and sanctions screening; confirm the commercial case | Commercial operations | 7 |
| 2. Agreement and legal review | Send the standard agreement; track redlines; confirm territory, exclusivity, pricing terms and term length | Legal and commercial lead | 21 |
| 3. Vendor registration and forms | Complete the partner's vendor forms and tax forms through the finance team; no bank or tax numbers are kept in commercial files | Finance and commercial operations | 10 |
| 4. Catalog and pricing sheet | Fill the partner sheet from the master (SKU, price, pack size, lead time) in the partner's own format; check against the master | Commercial operations | 12 |
| 5. Training and launch | Product and ordering training; confirm contacts; publish listings; place a test order | Sales and customer service | 7 |

## Exit criteria for launch
- Signed agreement on file.
- Vendor registration approved.
- Catalog audit shows zero mismatches against the master.
- Test order delivered and invoiced correctly.
- Named contacts on both sides: one decision-maker and one escalation contact.
