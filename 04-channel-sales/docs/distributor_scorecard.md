# Distributor scorecard

## Criteria and weights (100 points)
| Criterion | Points | How it is scored |
|---|---|---|
| Cold-chain capability | 30 | Can handle cryogenic shipping and import compliance (yes or no) |
| Portfolio fit | 25 | 1-5: do our products complement what they carry |
| Financial health | 20 | 1-5: stable, reputable, pays on time |
| Reach | 15 | 1-5: customer base and sales coverage in the territory |
| Own warehouse | 10 | Yes or no |
| Competitor overlap | -10 | Carries a direct competitor |

## Knockouts
Score is set to zero if the distributor is the **exclusive** agent for a competitor in the territory.

## Tiers
| Tier | Score | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 75 or more | First wave, personalised outreach |
| Tier 2 | 55-74 | Second wave |
| Tier 3 | below 55 | Hold, or serve through marketplaces |

## Rules
- Target regions: Europe and wealthy Asian markets; Canada selectively.
- One distributor per territory at first; do not contact a second until the first agreement concludes.
- Treat a group that owns several distributors as one conversation.
- One decision-maker plus at most one escalation contact per distributor; verify emails before sending.
- Log every touch in the CRM so reply rates by tier can be measured.
