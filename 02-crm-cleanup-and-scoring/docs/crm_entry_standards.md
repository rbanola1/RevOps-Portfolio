# CRM entry standards: objects, lead status and data upkeep

## Objects
| Object | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Company | The organisation that is a customer or prospect |
| Contact | Every individual person we communicate with |
| Pipeline | Where a prospect's progress through the sales steps is recorded |
| Product, quote, call, email, note, task, ticket | Supporting records attached to the objects above |

## Entry order
**Contact, then company, then deal.** Never create a deal directly when its company or contact does not exist yet; create them first, and create the deal inside the company.

Ways information enters the CRM: bulk upload from a spreadsheet or a lead database, one-to-one client emails (logged automatically), and manual entry.

## Lifecycle stage on manual entry
Lifecycle stages update automatically, so the person entering a contact only chooses a starting stage (lead, or sales qualified for a confirmed prospect). A potential distributor is marked as a distributor or channel partner, and no deal is created for them until a real order or requirement exists.

| Stage | Use |
|---|---|
| Subscriber | Subscribed to the newsletter; no sales interaction |
| Lead | Downloaded content or filled a form |
| MQL | Meets marketing criteria for sales readiness (score) |
| SQL | Sales has accepted the lead for active pursuit |
| Opportunity | A deal exists and is being worked |
| Customer | Closed-won deal |
| Evangelist | Customer who refers, gives case studies or testimonials |
| Other | Partners, vendors, employees |

## Lead status
| Status | When | Action |
|---|---|---|
| New | The lead just became SQL; no sales action yet | Assign to a rep immediately |
| Attempted to contact | A rep reached out, no response | Keep following up |
| Connected | The contact replied and is talking to sales | Resets automatically after 14 days with no progress |
| Open deal | Clear interest and a potential deal | Create the deal and move it through the pipeline |
| In progress | Being worked, not yet at deal stage | Keep nurturing and qualifying |
| Bad timing | Qualified but not ready to buy | Set a reminder to follow up later |
| Unqualified | Poor fit, or the person does not want to be contacted | Move to nurture or disqualify; select opt out of email when asked |

## Associations, notes and tasks
- Associate every deal with a company, the relevant contact and line items; use association labels so the role of each contact is clear.
- When a note records a conversation (a trade show, a call), associate it with the contact and the deal so it appears on each record's timeline.
- Mark tasks done when finished or move the due date; tasks exist for timely follow-up.

## Data upkeep checklist
1. Missing deal details break associations and weaken product and cash forecasts: fill every required field.
2. Check the company before attaching a deal; similar accounts exist for different departments.
3. Update deal close dates and stages regularly so junk and expired deals do not build up.
4. Mark tasks done or reschedule them.
5. Check the ticket section and close assigned tickets.
6. Update the contact source for trade show and conference contacts so event return can be measured.

## New user onboarding
A super admin creates the user; the user sets credentials and two-factor authentication (strongly recommended); the work mailbox is connected so emails log automatically; a short walkthrough video covers the contact, company, deal entry process.
