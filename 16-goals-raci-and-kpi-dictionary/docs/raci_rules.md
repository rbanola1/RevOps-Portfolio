# RACI rules

| Letter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R | Responsible: does the work |
| A | Accountable: owns the result and has the final say; exactly one per process |
| C | Consulted: gives input before a decision |
| I | Informed: told after a decision |

## Rules
- Each process has exactly one A; the script that builds the matrix fails if a process has none or more than one.
- A role may be both R and A; it is shown as A.
- Consulted roles are kept to those who can change the outcome.
- The matrix is reviewed every six months and whenever a role changes; it lives with the KPI dictionary.

## Examples
| Process | Accountable | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing and discount approval | CEO | Margin decision |
| Order to cash and invoicing | Finance | Cash and records |
| Lead scoring and lifecycle rules | Commercial operations | Shared definition of lead stages |
| Voice of customer to product | Product | Decides what to build |
| Territory and quota | CEO | Sets targets and pay |
