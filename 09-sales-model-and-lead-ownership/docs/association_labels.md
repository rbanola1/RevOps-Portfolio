# Association labels

Labels describe each contact's role on an account so that anyone opening a record knows who is who.

## Relationship-based
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Primary decision maker | Owns the buying decision |
| Department head or principal investigator | Leads the group or lab |
| End user | Uses the product |
| Lab manager | Runs day-to-day operations and ordering |
| Procurement contact | Handles purchase orders and supplier setup |
| Other stakeholder | Influences the decision |

Labels judged redundant (technical evaluator, budget approver) were dropped to keep the set short and consistently used.

## Product-specific
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Standard product contact | Interested in catalogue research products |
| Therapeutics contact | Collaboration or therapeutic interest |
| Custom service contact | Wants a custom project |
| Technical support | Needs application help |
| Multi-product interest | Interested in several lines |

## Rules
- One primary decision maker per account, and at most one escalation contact.
- Add a label whenever a contact is created or first engaged.
- Labels feed reporting, nurture segments and routing.
