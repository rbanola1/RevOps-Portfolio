# Ticketing setup

## Pipelines
| Pipeline | Contains | Does not contain |
|---|---|---|
| **Support** | Product-related inquiries and direct contact: pre-sale and post-sale support, collaboration inquiries, sales questions, complaints and negative feedback | General field information |
| **Voice of customer (VoC)** | Testimonials, product evaluations, general market and field information | Support issues |

Rule: if it concerns how a customer uses or experiences our product, and it needs action, it is a support ticket. Testimonials are the one case where product use belongs in VoC.

## How tickets are created
1. **Email** to the support inbox or any monitored team inbox.
2. **Website contact form** with an inquiry type chosen by the customer.
3. **Phone** calls to customer service or technical support create a ticket automatically.
4. **Manual**: any team member creates a ticket from the CRM.

Not ticketed: careers and investor inquiries, which go to the relevant mailbox directly.

## Inquiry categories, owners and alerts
| Inquiry category | Inbox | Owner team | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product sales inquiries | sales | Sales | Sales Inquiry |
| General | customer service | Commercial operations | General |
| Customer service and orders | customer service | Commercial operations | CS / Orders |
| Custom differentiation and new cell development | sales | Sales | Custom / New Cell |
| Therapeutics collaborations | sales | Business development | Therapeutics |
| Technical support and applications | support | Field applications and sales | Technical Support |

Alerts: team inbox, the assigned owner's personal inbox and in-app notifications. The service desk stays open during working hours so replies are timely.

## Ticket categories (for reporting)
Pre-sale support · order issue · usage and protocol support · documentation request · large-scale or custom order request · quality and safety concern · shipping or handling concern · testimonial · troubleshooting · accounting · spam · not applicable.
Choose one category; two only when truly necessary.

## Ticket fields
Description · owner (required) · priority · ticket message (form text) · inquiry category (required) · ticket category (required) · refund or replacement (required: refund, replacement or N/A) · order, lot or tracking number · product group (required) · item (required) · notes for every interaction (one note per interaction, so dates are tracked).

## Statuses (automatic where possible)
| Status | Set when |
|---|---|
| New | Ticket created |
| Waiting on us | Customer replies |
| Waiting on contact | We send a reply |
| Closed | Resolved; triggers the satisfaction survey |

If we reply but still owe a follow-up, switch the ticket back to "waiting on us" by hand. All required fields are filled before a ticket leaves "New".

## Service levels
| Target | Value |
|---|---|
| First response to new or ongoing ticket | within 24 hours |
| Ideal time to close | 72 hours |
| Nature | Internal expectation, tracked in the CRM, not promised to the customer |

Scripted question lists guide technical support so answers are consistent.

## Naming convention
`<customer> - <product> - <order or lot number> <type>`, for example "Northbridge Lab - Endothelial Cells - #1243 Refund".

## Measurement
Ticket volume by category (excluding spam), first-response SLA met, time to close, backlog by status, customer effort score from the survey sent on closure, tickets per product group for voice-of-customer review.
