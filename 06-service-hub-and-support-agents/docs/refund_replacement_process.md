# Refund and replacement process

## Principles
1. **Investigate first.** Collect experiment details, controls, protocol, photos and lot or batch information, and document them in the ticket.
2. **Rule out user error.** If the product was used incorrectly, guide the customer and close the ticket; replacements are generally not issued.
3. **Verify before replacing.** Ask for a repeat experiment with controls, or review internal batch data.
4. **Replacement is the preferred resolution**, not a refund.
5. **Stage large orders.** Send a small replacement from the same lot first, confirm with an experiment, and ask R&D to test the batch internally case by case.
6. **Refunds are rare.** They need management approval and are documented in the ticket.

## Refund routes
| Payment type | Route |
|---|---|
| Prepaid orders (online store) | Management approval, then the web team issues the refund in the store |
| Other payments (bank transfer, cheque, net terms) | Management approval, then email finance to refund according to the customer's preference, copying management |
| Delivered but unpaid | If approved, issue a zero-dollar invoice instead |

## Ticketing the refund
- Create the ticket **inside the existing deal**. Do not move the deal from closed won to closed lost, because the order was delivered and the refund may come later.
- The ticket tracks refund amounts for later reporting.
- Associate it with the correct contact and company; fill category "confirmed product performance issue", product group, item, source and the refund or replacement choice.
- Use the naming convention and upload order information in notes.

## Replacement steps
Management approval, mark the ticket, create an internal order at zero dollars, release to operations, fulfil, close the ticket with tracking, and set a follow-up task 2-3 weeks later.
