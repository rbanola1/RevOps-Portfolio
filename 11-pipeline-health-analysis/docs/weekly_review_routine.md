# Weekly pipeline review routine

**When:** Monday, 30 minutes. **Who:** sales reps, sales manager, commercial operations.

| Step | Minutes | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Stalled list | 10 | Which deals are past benchmark or idle 21+ days, and what is the next step for each? | Each stalled deal gets an action and a date, or is closed as lost |
| 2. Coverage | 5 | Is open pipeline at least 3x next-quarter quota, with and without stalled deals? | Gap in dollars and in number of deals |
| 3. New pipeline | 5 | Which sources added deals last week, and how does it compare with the plan? | Shortfall passed to marketing and outbound |
| 4. Forecast | 5 | Does weighted pipeline agree with the forecast range? | Forecast change, if any, with the reason |
| 5. Data quality | 5 | Which deals lack amount, close date or next step? | List sent to owners; fixed before Friday |

## Rules
- The report is generated from the CRM; nobody builds it by hand.
- A deal discussed twice with no change in its next step is a candidate to close.
- Changes in amount or close date need a reason in the deal notes.
