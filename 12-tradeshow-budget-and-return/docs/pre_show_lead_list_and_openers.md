# Pre-show lead list and personalised openers

## Goal
Book meetings before the show by building a short, targeted list from the attendee and poster information and writing a specific opener for each person. All names below are invented.

## Method
| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1. Collect | Pull the exhibitor, speaker and poster lists and the registered-attendee list where available |
| 2. Suppress | Remove existing customers, people on do-not-contact lists and people already in active conversations |
| 3. Score | Score each person on segment fit, role, product relevance of their poster or talk, and past engagement |
| 4. Tier | Tier A (top 25%): personal opener and a meeting request; Tier B: short note with a booth invitation; Tier C: show announcement only |
| 5. Enrich | Fill missing contact details for tiers A and B only |
| 6. Write openers | One specific sentence about the person's poster, talk or recent work, then one sentence of relevance and one clear ask |
| 7. Send and track | Send two weeks, one week and two days before the show; log replies and booked meetings |
| 8. After the show | Follow up within 48 hours; record leads, deals and feedback against the event |

## Example openers (invented people)
| Tier | Opener |
|---|---|
| A | "Dr. Example, your poster on vascular differentiation caught my eye. We supply a defined medium used in similar protocols. Could we meet for 15 minutes at booth 120 on Tuesday?" |
| B | "We will be at booth 120 with data on endothelial expansion. If vascular models are part of your work, stop by for a short demonstration." |
| C | "Aurelis will exhibit at the meeting; details and times are on our events page." |

## Rules
- Fewer than 150 sends per show; fit before volume.
- No attachments in the first message; one link at most.
- Stop after two follow-ups.
- Meetings booked, show-day leads and deals are tracked against the event budget and return model in this project.

## Measures
| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Reply rate, tier A | 10% or more |
| Meetings booked before the show | 15 to 25 |
| Share of show leads that came from pre-booked meetings | 30% or more |
