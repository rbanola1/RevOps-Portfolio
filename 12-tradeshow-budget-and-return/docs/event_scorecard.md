# Event scorecard

| Measure | Weight | How measured |
|---|---|---|
| Gross-profit return per $1 spent | 50% | First-year revenue x gross margin / total cost |
| Cost per qualified lead | 30% | Total cost / qualified leads; lower is better |
| Audience fit | 20% | Share of attendees matching the target customer profile, from the organiser's attendee data and past events |

## Decision rule
| Gross-profit return | Decision | Typical action |
|---|---|---|
| 1.2x or more | Repeat | Keep or grow; secure the booth early |
| 0.8x to 1.2x | Change | Smaller booth, a talk or poster, a meeting-led approach, or fewer staff |
| Below 0.8x | Drop | Replace with a webinar, a partner event or a regional meeting |

## Other checks
- Useful customer intelligence per entry, from the voice-of-customer program.
- Competitor presence and share of target accounts attending.
- Distributor meetings held at the event.
