# Review cadence

## Monthly
1. Pull revenue, wins, deals and SQLs by channel for the month and year to date.
2. Compare to plan to date (plan x share of year elapsed, adjusted for known seasonality such as event dates).
3. Rank channels by variance in dollars, not percent.
4. For each large gap ask which step of the funnel is weak: volume, qualification, close rate or order size.
5. Re-forecast the year using the current run-rate and the remaining event calendar.
6. Decide: add budget, cut, change message, or fix a process step.

## Quarterly
- Re-estimate channel conversion rates and costs from actual data.
- Re-run the capacity check and the sensitivity table.
- Retire or reduce channels with low return that do not serve another purpose (awareness, relationships).

## Report to leadership
One page: target, year to date versus plan, run-rate forecast, top three gaps with causes, and the decisions needed.
