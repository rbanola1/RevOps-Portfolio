# Action loop

| Theme | Owner | Typical action | Reported back at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product performance | Product management and R&D | Investigate patterns by product and lot; update protocols | Monthly review |
| Documentation | Operations and technical support | Fix or add documents, certificates and instructions | Monthly review |
| Pricing and quotes | Commercial operations | Review quote turnaround, discount rules, bundles | Quarterly pricing review |
| Delivery and logistics | Operations | Packaging, carrier or timing changes | Monthly review |
| Application support | Technical support | Add protocol notes, FAQs or webinars | Monthly review |
| Competitor information | Commercial operations and marketing | Update the competitor file and comparison sheets | Quarterly |
| Product request | Product management | Rank requests; decide build, partner or decline | Quarterly roadmap meeting |
| Testimonial | Marketing | Request permission, write a short case, use in outreach and web | After permission |
| Objection: evidence or data | Marketing and R&D | Build the data pack or reference that answers it | Monthly review |

## Rules
- Every record has an owner and a next step; a record without an owner is a quality failure.
- Ranked requests and objections go to the owners monthly, with counts and examples.
- The owner replies with a decision (act, schedule, decline) and the reason, and the team tells the customer where appropriate.
- Close the loop with customers who gave feedback that led to a change; it builds loyalty and more feedback.
