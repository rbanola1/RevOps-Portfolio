# Monthly close and review

## Close checklist (working days 1-5)
1. Orders shipped and invoiced in accounting; credits and adjustments posted.
2. CRM deals closed won this month have company, close date and product; fix gaps.
3. Reconcile CRM closed-won to invoiced revenue with the bridge.
4. Update actuals in the model and refresh the dashboards.

## Variance review (working day 6)
- Rank P&L lines by dollar variance.
- Explain every variance above a threshold (for example 5% and $5K): volume, price, mix, timing or one-off.
- Mark each as favorable or unfavorable and whether it will recur.

## Forecast update
- Replace the forecast months with actuals as they close.
- Refresh weighted pipeline and run-rate.
- Re-run scenarios and the cash projection.

## Review meeting
One page: year to date versus budget, full-year forecast versus budget, cash position, top three drivers of the gap, decisions needed (spend, hiring, channel focus).

## Controls
- One owner per data source: product and price in the product database, orders in the ERP, invoices in accounting, deals in the CRM.
- Keep the original budget frozen; changes go into a labelled reforecast version.
- Keep a log of assumption changes with date and reason.
