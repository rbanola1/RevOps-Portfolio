# Budget method

## Structure
| Block | How it is built |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Annual target split by route (direct 78%, distributor 12%, marketplace 10%), phased by monthly seasonality and each route's ramp |
| Cost of goods | Revenue by route times (1 minus gross margin by route): direct 62%, distributor 42%, marketplace 55% |
| Operating expenses | Salaries and benefits, marketing and events, software and systems, shipping and fulfilment, travel, professional services, other |
| Seasonality | Event-driven calendar: lower in winter, higher in autumn; marketing, travel and shipping follow it |

## Steps
1. Set the revenue target from the lead generation plan (reverse funnel), not from last year plus a percentage.
2. Allocate by route using expected contribution and ramp time. Slow-ramp routes (distributors) start later in the year.
3. Apply route gross margins; distributors carry lower margin because of their discount.
4. Build operating expenses bottom-up: headcount first, then programme spend tied to the lead generation plan, then fixed costs.
5. Phase monthly.
6. Review with finance and the commercial team; lock a version and record the assumptions.

## Good practice
- Keep a one-page assumptions sheet: order value, win rates, ramp, margins, headcount, events.
- Budget cash as well as P&L; collections differ by route.
- Reforecast quarterly and keep the original budget unchanged for variance reporting.
